About Matchday Soccer Planet

Matchday Soccer Planet is an independent South African football website providing original analysis, Betway Premiership coverage, FIFA World Cup 2026 reporting, transfer news and a growing community of SA football fans. The site is proudly based in Ga-Rankuwa, Pretoria, and this year marks our first anniversary of independent football coverage.
