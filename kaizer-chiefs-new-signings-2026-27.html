Kaizer Chiefs New Signings 2026/27: Every Arrival and Departure

Fernando Da Cruz's rebuild in full — four confirmed signings, a reshaped goalkeeping department, and a group of DDC graduates fighting for their chance.

📅 26 July 2026 ✍️ Kgomotso Nigel Peu, Independent Sports Journalist 🏷️ Kaizer Chiefs, Transfers, Betway Premiership ⏱️ 6 min read

Kaizer Chiefs enter the 2026/27 Betway Premiership season under a new permanent head coach, Fernando Da Cruz, following a transfer window that has so far produced four confirmed signings and five departures. It has not been the busiest window in the Betway Premiership, but Amakhosi's recruitment has been targeted, addressing specific problem positions identified in an end-of-season technical review after last season's third-place finish.

Who's the Most Valuable Addition?

On reputation and readiness alone, Thabo Moloisane and Khanyisa Mayo stand out as the two most significant arrivals. Moloisane arrives as an experienced, proven Betway Premiership performer with continental football experience from his time at Stellenbosch, giving Da Cruz a composed, leadership-minded option at the back — an area the club's own technical review flagged as needing more solidity. Mayo, meanwhile, is a striker who has already proven his goal instincts in South African colours during his loan spell, and converting that into a permanent deal removes any uncertainty over his long-term future at Naturena heading into the new season.

Who's Set to Make an Immediate Impact?

Adolf Mtasingwa Bitegeko is the name most likely to surprise neutral observers. The Tanzanian international's move from the Tanzanian Premier League has already drawn glowing endorsements from former players and coaches who believe he has the profile to slot straight into Da Cruz's midfield. Combined with Mayo's proven scoring touch, Chiefs' front two recruitment areas look the most likely to deliver quick returns, even if the squad's overall depth still trails Orlando Pirates and Mamelodi Sundowns.

Confirmed Departures

Chiefs have confirmed five departures alongside their new arrivals. Goalkeeper Fiacre Ntwari has joined AmaZulu FC on loan, opening the door for Leaner's arrival, while midfielders George Matlou and Happy Mashiane have both left the club. Striker Tashreeq Morris also departed earlier in the window, and academy graduate Aden McCarthy has completed a move to Europe, bringing his time at Naturena to an end. Several further exits — including Gaston Sirino, Sibongiseni Mthethwa, Pule Mmodi and Ashley Du Preez — have been widely linked but not yet confirmed as this window continues.

Don't Forget the Academy: Four DDC Graduates in the Mix

Away from the senior transfer market, Chiefs have also promoted four players from their DStv Diski Challenge side to first-team contention: Pheko "Fabregas" Phago and goalkeeper Takalani Madzhamba, both of whom made their senior debuts as substitutes against Chippa United in the final match of last season, alongside Xolani Cossa, who spent last term on loan at Black Lions FC in Botswana, and teenage striker Naledi Hlongwane, who has already been handed the No. 35 shirt. It ultimately falls to Da Cruz to decide how many of that group remain involved with the first team rather than being sent out on development loans, but their inclusion continues a pipeline that has already produced regular starters such as Mduduzi Shabalala and Wandile Duba.

What's Next

Former Chiefs co-coach Khalil Ben Youssef's end-of-season technical report reportedly recommended reinforcements in five areas: a left winger, left-back, central defender, midfielder and goalkeeper. With three of those five positions already addressed, Chiefs are expected to remain active before the transfer window closes on 22 September, with a left winger and attacking midfielder the most likely remaining priorities as Da Cruz prepares his squad to open the season away at newly promoted Kruger United.
