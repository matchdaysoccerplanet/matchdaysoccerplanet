SA Sports Jobs: Every Vacancy Closing in August & September 2026

Government sport departments, campus sport, betting and sports media, and club management — a round-up of active South African sports-sector vacancies with closing dates this August and September.

📅 6 August 2026 ✍️ Matchday Soccer Planet Editorial 🏷️ Careers, DSAC, Betway, Sports Management ⏱️ 7 min read

South Africa's sports economy stretches well beyond the touchline — from the government departments that fund and regulate it, to the betting and media companies that market it, to the clubs and campuses that run it day to day. Right now, several roles across that ecosystem have active closing dates in August and September 2026. Here's a full round-up of what's open, where, and how to apply.

Public Sector & Government Sports Departments

National Department of Sport, Arts and Culture (DSAC)
Roles: Monitoring & Evaluation Officers, Operations Management Analysts, Data Management Specialists · Location: Pretoria / National offices

Advertised via the Public Service Vacancy Circular, these posts call for a National Diploma or Degree (NQF Level 6/7) in Public Administration, Statistics, Data Management, or a related field.

How to apply: Online via the DSAC E-Recruitment portal (erecruit.dsac.gov.za), using the electronic Z83 form.

Closes 07 August 2026, 16:00
Limpopo Department of Sport, Arts and Culture
Roles: Public Sports & Recreation Administration · Location: Limpopo Province

A provincial-level opportunity for those looking to work in sports and recreation administration at government level.

How to apply: Provincial Z83 form submissions, or via the provincial e-portal.

Extended closing date: 24 August 2026

Higher Education & Campus Sports

Newbridge Graduate Institute — Sport and Social Coordinator
Location: Western Cape

The role covers managing campus sports events, driving student participation, and creating sports-related social media content. Candidates need NQF Level 6 in Sports Management, PR, or Events, plus two years' experience.

How to apply: Via MyJobMag South Africa or directly on the institute's careers page.

Closing mid-to-late August 2026

Sports Media, Betting Tech & Digital Marketing

Initiate Recruitment / Sports Tech Partners
Roles: Social Media Specialist (Sports & Betting Culture), Digital Designer, Online Media Buyer · Location: Johannesburg & Cape Town

These posts focus on content creation, campaign strategy, and audience engagement across digital sports platforms and media networks.

How to apply: Listed on Indeed South Africa and LinkedIn Jobs.

Rolling applications, August–September 2026
Betway Group — Brand Lead (International) & Senior Social Media Designer
Location: Johannesburg / Cape Town

Focused on regional sports brand campaigns, media partnership execution, and visual assets for live sports coverage.

How to apply: Directly via the Betway Careers portal or LinkedIn.

Applications open through August 2026
Balderstone Sports Institute / Rubicomm — Digital Marketing & Graphic Design Specialist
Location: Pretoria / Johannesburg, Gauteng

A design- and marketing-focused role within the sports institute and communications space.

How to apply: Listed via SimplyHired ZA and Indeed.

Closing mid-August 2026

Sports Club & Facility Management

Country Club Johannesburg (CCJ) — Assistant Sports Manager & Racquets Manager
Location: Johannesburg, Gauteng

Duties include overseeing sports facilities, organising tournaments, and managing sports coaching staff at one of Johannesburg's established private clubs.

How to apply: Via Adzuna ZA or direct submission to CCJ HR.

Closing mid-August 2026

Key Dates at a Glance

07 Aug 2026
DSAC national vacancies (M&E Officers, Operations Analysts, Data Specialists) close at 16:00.
Mid-Aug 2026
Newbridge Graduate Institute, Balderstone/Rubicomm, and Country Club Johannesburg roles are expected to close.
24 Aug 2026
Limpopo Department of Sport, Arts and Culture's extended closing date for public sports and recreation administration posts.
Aug–Sep 2026
Betway Group and Initiate Recruitment / Sports Tech Partners roles remain open on a rolling basis.
Before you apply: Always confirm the current closing date and application requirements directly on the employer's official careers portal or the relevant government e-recruitment site before submitting, as vacancy circulars and rolling listings can be updated or withdrawn.
