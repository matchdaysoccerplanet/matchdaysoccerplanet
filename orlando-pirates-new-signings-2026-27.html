Orlando Pirates New Signings 2026/27: Every Arrival and Departure

Fresh from a historic treble, the Buccaneers have made eight signings and seven exits — while six DDC champions train with the first team.

📅 26 July 2026 ✍️ Kgomotso Nigel Peu, Independent Sports Journalist 🏷️ Orlando Pirates, Transfers, Betway Premiership ⏱️ 6 min read

Orlando Pirates enter the 2026/27 season as reigning Betway Premiership champions after last season's historic domestic treble, and head coach Abdeslam Ouaddou has continued to reshape his squad accordingly. The Buccaneers have confirmed eight new signings and seven departures so far this transfer window, while also blooding six graduates from a record-breaking DStv Diski Challenge title-winning side into first-team training.

Who's the Most Valuable Addition?

Neo Rapoo stands out as the most talked-about arrival, and for good reason. Orlando Pirates themselves described his signing as "particularly significant," with the club's official statement praising his energy, technical quality and significant potential as a modern full-back comfortable both defending and advancing into attacking areas. As a product of SuperSport United's development structures with South Africa youth international honours already to his name, Rapoo arrives with a higher profile than most 20-year-old defensive signings in the Betway Premiership.

Who's Set to Make an Immediate Impact?

Matome Mmolai and Mthetheleli Mthiyane both arrive with a specific mandate: adding energy and technical quality to a midfield that must now function without Relebohile Mofokeng, who completed his move to Belgian side Royale Union Saint-Gilloise earlier this month. Mmolai, described as comfortable in possession and capable of contributing across different phases of play, could be the more instant fit given his experience level, while Mthiyane's arrival from Stellenbosch gives Ouaddou a proven Betway Premiership performer to partner Thalente Mbatha in the engine room.

Confirmed Departures

Pirates have confirmed the departure of seven squad members. Defenders Gomolemo Khoto and Siyabonga Ndlozi have completed permanent transfers to Sekhukhune United, while right-back Tshepho Mashiloane returns there on a season-long loan. Fan favourites Sipho Mbule and Thabiso Lebitso were among the more surprising exits — Lebitso lost his place after a long-term injury allowed Deano van Rooyen and Kamohelo Sebelebele to establish themselves at right-back, while Mbule had featured regularly and played a key role in Bafana Bafana's World Cup qualification before his own departure.

Don't Forget the Academy: Six DDC Champions in First-Team Training

Beyond the senior transfer market, Pirates have opened the door to six graduates of their record-breaking DStv Diski Challenge side, which won the 2025/26 title with 75 points — breaking the previous competition record of 70 points held by Kaizer Chiefs. Defenders Cristian Derbyshire and Mpho Mphekgoane are being assessed as central defensive options, Tshepo Mhlambi provides cover at left-back, and midfielders Simphiwe Masilela, Lebohang Mohlale and Lungelo Dondolo round out the group. It is a pathway that has already produced Mbekezeli Mbokazi, Relebohile Mofokeng and Mohau Nkota in recent seasons, and Ouaddou is expected to monitor all six closely, potentially taking some of the group on the club's pre-season trip to Spain.

What's Next

With Mofokeng's departure leaving a creative gap to fill, Pirates' recruitment focus for the remainder of the window is expected to shift toward proven attacking output rather than further squad depth. Ouaddou's side begin the defence of their Betway Premiership title with a squad reshaped for competition on three fronts: the league, the Nedbank Cup and MTN8 defence, and a maiden campaign in the CAF Champions League as African qualifiers.

Managing Success and Turnover Together

Rebuilding a treble-winning squad is a delicate balancing act. Ouaddou must integrate eight new faces without disrupting the tactical cohesion and dressing-room chemistry that delivered an MTN8, Carling Knockout Cup and Betway Premiership title in his very first season in charge. History suggests South African clubs that win major honours often struggle to retain the same level the following season, precisely because of the churn a successful campaign generates in transfer interest, player expectations and squad harmony. How quickly Ouaddou can settle his new arrivals into a settled first-choice XI may matter more to Pirates' title defence than the individual quality of any single signing.

A Continental Test Awaits

Adding further complexity to the rebuild, Pirates enter the 2026/27 season with a maiden CAF Champions League campaign to prepare for as African qualifiers, on top of their usual domestic cup commitments. Squad depth that looked more than sufficient purely for a Betway Premiership title challenge will be tested far more severely by a season that could ultimately include continental fixtures stretching well into 2027, making this transfer window's business — and any further additions before the deadline — considerably higher-stakes than in a normal season.
