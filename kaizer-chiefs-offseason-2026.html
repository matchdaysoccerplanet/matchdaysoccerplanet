PSL Off-Season: Kaizer Chiefs Coaching Search, Promotions and 2026/27 Preview

With the 2025/26 season wrapped up, we look at coaching changes, the promotion playoffs, and what next season could bring.

📅 10 June 2026 ✍️ Matchday Soccer Planet Editorial 🏷️ PSL, Kaizer Chiefs ⏱️ 4 min read

Kaizer Chiefs confirmed the departure of interim co-coaches Cedric Kaze and Khalil Ben Youssef at the end of the 2025/26 season, closing the book on an interim arrangement that steadied the ship but was always intended to be temporary. The pair guided Amakhosi to third place in the Betway Premiership and secured CAF Confederation Cup qualification — a respectable return, but not enough to earn either man the job on a permanent basis.

Fernando Da Cruz Takes Charge

Chiefs' search for a permanent successor ultimately landed on Fernando Da Cruz, who was confirmed as head coach ahead of the 2026/27 campaign. Da Cruz inherits a squad in the middle of a significant rebuild, with Sporting Director Kaizer Motaung Jr overseeing a transfer window that has brought in defender Thabo Moloisane, goalkeeper Renaldo Leaner, Tanzanian international midfielder Adolf Mtasingwa Bitegeko, and striker Khanyisa Mayo on a permanent deal after his loan spell. Chiefs' hierarchy is under pressure to make the new appointment count quickly — the club's fanbase, among the largest and most vocal in South African football, has grown increasingly impatient with a period of coaching instability that has coincided with the club's longest trophy drought in its history.

Da Cruz inherits a squad that finished a credible third but still trails both Orlando Pirates and Mamelodi Sundowns by a significant points margin — meaning his primary mandate will be closing that gap rather than simply consolidating on last season's finish. Chiefs also took their new-look squad to Spain for an intensive pre-season camp, giving Da Cruz an early opportunity to assess his options before the season kicks off away to newly promoted Kruger United.

The Promotion Playoffs

At the other end of the table, relegated side Orbit College face a challenging route back to the top flight, entering the promotion playoffs against Magesi FC, Cape Town City FC, and Milford FC. The playoff format means Orbit College's Betway Premiership status is anything but guaranteed to return quickly, and all three of their rivals will fancy their chances of claiming the solitary promotion spot on offer.

What to Watch in 2026/27

Beyond Chiefs' coaching decision, the new Betway Premiership season arrives with Orlando Pirates defending a historic treble, Mamelodi Sundowns balancing continental ambitions after their CAF Champions League triumph, and a host of clubs looking to close the gap on South Africa's traditional "Big Three." With several breakout stars from the 2025/26 season already attracting European interest, squad retention will be as important a storyline as results on the pitch heading into the new campaign, which is expected to kick off in August 2026.

Chiefs' Academy Pipeline Also in Focus

Away from the senior transfer market, Chiefs have also promoted a group of DStv Diski Challenge graduates into first-team contention, including Pheko "Fabregas" Phago and goalkeeper Takalani Madzhamba, both of whom made their senior debuts in the final match of last season, alongside Xolani Cossa and teenage striker Naledi Hlongwane. Da Cruz will ultimately decide how many of that group stay involved with the first team rather than heading out on development loans, but their promotion continues a pipeline that has already produced regular starters such as Mduduzi Shabalala and Wandile Duba in recent seasons.

For a club of Chiefs' size and history, patience is in short supply. Da Cruz will be judged not just on results but on whether Naturena can finally break the trophy drought that has defined the last decade, and the opening weeks of the new season will set the tone for how much room he is given to build.
