2025/26 PSL Awards Tonight: Every Nominee, the Favourites, and How to Watch

South African football's biggest individual honours are handed out this evening. Here's who's nominated, who could stand out, and everything you need to watch it live.

📅 Published 27 July 2026 · Updated 27 July 2026 ✍️ Kgomotso Nigel Peu, Founder & Independent Sports Journalist 🏷️ Betway Premiership, PSL Awards ⏱️ 8 min read
📌 Preview, not results: the 2025/26 PSL Awards ceremony takes place this evening. This article previews the confirmed nominees and context ahead of the event — no winners have been announced at the time of publication. Check back on Matchday Soccer Planet after the ceremony for the full results.

South African football's individual honours night has arrived. The Premier Soccer League (PSL) will crown the winners of the 2025/26 PSL Awards this evening, Monday, 27 July 2026, at a ceremony recognising the standout performers from the Betway Premiership, the Motsepe Foundation Championship, the DStv Diski Challenge and the season's cup competitions. With nominees confirmed across every major category, here's everything South African football fans need to know before tonight's show gets under way.

The PSL confirmed the broadcast details on its own channels, inviting fans to "celebrate the stars of South African football" live as the winners are crowned across every category.

Why This Season's Awards Carry Extra Weight

Context matters heading into tonight's ceremony. Orlando Pirates ended Mamelodi Sundowns' eight-year grip on the Betway Premiership title, winning it on the final day of the season with a 2-0 victory over relegated Orbit College — a result that also handed the Buccaneers a domestic treble alongside the MTN8 and Carling Knockout Cup. That success is reflected directly in tonight's nominee list, with Pirates comfortably the most-nominated club across the board. Mamelodi Sundowns, despite falling short domestically, won the biggest prize on the continent, lifting the CAF Champions League — a trophy that sits outside the PSL Awards' own categories but shapes how this season is remembered regardless of tonight's results. Kaizer Chiefs, meanwhile, endured another trophyless campaign, though a third-place finish — their best league position since 2019/20 — was enough to secure CAF Confederation Cup qualification for next season.

PSL Footballer of the Season

  • Oswin Appollis — Orlando Pirates
  • Relebohile Mofokeng — Orlando Pirates
  • Teboho Mokoena — Mamelodi Sundowns
Voted for exclusively by the 16 Betway Premiership head coaches; coaches cannot vote for their own players.

This is the night's headline category, and it doubles as a personal farewell for Relebohile Mofokeng, who completed his reported R70 million move to Belgian side Royale Union Saint-Gilloise earlier this month. Mofokeng arrives at the ceremony as the most-nominated individual across the entire night with four separate nominations, and pundits widely expect him to be among the evening's biggest winners even though he is no longer a Betway Premiership player. His Pirates teammate Oswin Appollis, however, enjoyed arguably the more complete all-round season and cannot be discounted, while Sundowns' Teboho Mokoena — whose composed World Cup penalty against Czechia will still be fresh in many voters' minds — offers the category a genuine alternative outside the treble-winning Pirates camp.

Betway Premiership Players' Player of the Season

  • Oswin Appollis — Orlando Pirates
  • Relebohile Mofokeng — Orlando Pirates
  • Kamogelo Sebelebele — Orlando Pirates
Voted for exclusively by Betway Premiership players themselves; each club had a maximum of 30 votes and no player could nominate or vote for their own teammate.

An all-Pirates shortlist here says everything about how the Buccaneers' peers around the league viewed their title-winning campaign. That this award is decided by fellow professionals rather than coaches or journalists makes it one of the most respected honours of the night.

Betway Premiership Coach of the Season

  • Miguel Cardoso — Mamelodi Sundowns
  • Cedric Kaze & Khalil Ben Youssef — Kaizer Chiefs (interim co-coaches)
  • Abdeslam Ouaddou — Orlando Pirates

Abdeslam Ouaddou goes in as the clear favourite after delivering a domestic treble in his very first season at Mayfair, matching a feat last achieved by club legend Ruud Krol in 2011. Cardoso's continental achievement with Sundowns offers the strongest counter-argument, even though it took place outside the Betway Premiership itself, while Chiefs' interim pairing of Kaze and Ben Youssef guided Amakhosi to their best league finish in six seasons — a quieter, but genuine, coaching achievement of its own.

Beyond these headline categories, the PSL also confirmed nominees drawn from the Motsepe Foundation Championship and match officials, voted for separately by the SAFA National Referees Committee Panel, while all other individual categories not decided by coaches or players are judged by a panel of football journalists, broadcasters, analysts and commentators.

What Readers Might Be Missing

Mofokeng's nominations arrive after he's already left the league. It's a reminder of how PSL Awards timelines work: this ceremony honours the 2025/26 season in full, meaning a player can collect nominations, and potentially trophies, for performances delivered for a club he no longer plays for.

Sundowns' continental triumph sits outside these categories. Because the CAF Champions League is not a PSL competition, Sundowns' biggest trophy of the year won't be directly reflected on the trophy table tonight, even though it will likely be referenced throughout the ceremony as one of the season's landmark achievements for South African football.

Kaizer Chiefs go into the night without a trophy nomination of note beyond the Goalkeeper of the Season and Goal of the Season shortlists — a quiet reminder of the decade-long silverware drought the club is still working to end, even after a promising start under new coach Fernando Da Cruz in this week's Toyota Cup.

The voting process itself varies by category — some awards are decided by coaches, others exclusively by players, others by match officials, and the remainder by an independent panel of media professionals — which is worth bearing in mind before assuming any single group of insiders picked every winner.

Who Might Stand Out Tonight

If pushed to identify the two players most likely to leave with multiple trophies, Relebohile Mofokeng and Oswin Appollis are the obvious starting point given the sheer breadth of their nominations and Orlando Pirates' treble-winning campaign. Abdeslam Ouaddou looks the strong favourite for Coach of the Season given the scale of what he achieved in his debut year at the club. None of this is a guaranteed outcome, however — Teboho Mokoena's World Cup exploits and Miguel Cardoso's continental triumph both give voters legitimate reasons to look beyond Mayfair for at least some of tonight's biggest honours.
