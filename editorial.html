Editorial Policy

Matchday Soccer Planet is committed to accurate, fair, and original football journalism covering South African and international football. This Editorial Policy sets out the standards our reporting is held to.

Our Standards

All articles published on this site are original work, researched and written by our editorial team rather than copied or lightly rewritten from other outlets. Where we reference statistics, quotes or factual claims sourced from other credible outlets — such as official club statements, SAFA communications, or established South African and international sports media — we credit those sources within the article and avoid reproducing their original wording beyond short, clearly necessary excerpts.

Fact-Checking Process

Before publication, we cross-reference key facts — scorelines, squad details, transfer fees, dates and direct quotes — against multiple independent sources wherever possible, favouring official club and federation statements, established South African sports outlets, and confirmed on-the-record comments over speculation or unverified social media reports. Where information remains genuinely unconfirmed at the time of writing — such as a player's next transfer destination — we say so explicitly rather than presenting speculation as fact.

Corrections

We take factual accuracy seriously and will correct any genuine error promptly and transparently once identified. If you believe any article on this site contains a factual mistake, please report it to matchdaysoccerplanet@gmail.com with as much detail as possible, and we will investigate and correct it where warranted.

Independence

Our editorial content is produced independently of any club, federation, sponsor or advertiser. Sponsored content, where it exists, is always clearly labelled as such and kept separate from our news and analysis coverage. Advertising displayed on this site through Google AdSense does not influence our editorial judgement or story selection.

Author Attribution

Articles are bylined to their author, giving readers a clear sense of who is responsible for the reporting and analysis they are reading. Our founder and lead writer, Kgomotso Nigel Peu, can be reached via the Contact details on our About page for any editorial queries.
