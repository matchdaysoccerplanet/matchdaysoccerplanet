BREAKING: Mofokeng Signs for Royale Union Saint-Gilloise in R70 Million Deal

South Africa's brightest young talent completes a blockbuster move to Belgium — flying straight from Los Angeles to Brussels after Bafana's World Cup exit.

📅 3 July 2026 ✍️ Matchday Soccer Planet Editorial 🏷️ Transfers, Orlando Pirates, Bafana Bafana ⏱️ 4 min read

The move South African football fans had been waiting for is official. Relebohile Mofokeng, 21, has signed a four-year deal with Belgian Pro League club Royale Union Saint-Gilloise, with the transfer fee reported by SABC Sport to be over R70 million. He will wear the No. 38 jersey — the same number he made famous at Orlando Pirates.

A Secret Deal, Kept Quiet Through the World Cup

In a remarkable touch, Mofokeng did not fly home with his Bafana Bafana teammates after the Round of 32 defeat to Canada in Los Angeles on 28 June. Instead, he boarded a flight directly to Brussels to complete his medical and sign personal terms. Orlando Pirates confirmed the deal had actually been agreed before the World Cup, but both clubs deliberately kept it secret to avoid distracting Mofokeng during the tournament — a decision that speaks to how highly both parties valued his focus on representing his country first.

At his unveiling, Mofokeng said he was thrilled to fulfil his dream of playing in Europe and described the past year — the league title, his first FIFA World Cup appearance, and now this move — as a "very special" one.

Following in Percy Tau's Footsteps

The move follows a well-trodden path for South African talent heading to Europe. Percy Tau also used Royale Union Saint-Gilloise as a springboard before moving on to Club Brugge, Anderlecht, and eventually the English Premier League with Brighton. For South African football, Mofokeng's transfer is another sign of a generation growing in confidence and ambition after Bafana Bafana's historic World Cup campaign, in which the team reached the knockout stage for the first time in the nation's history.

What He Leaves Behind at Pirates

Mofokeng made 125 appearances for Orlando Pirates, scoring 29 goals and providing 31 assists — numbers that made him one of the most influential attacking players in the Betway Premiership over the past two seasons. He was central to the club's historic domestic treble under head coach Abdeslam Ouaddou in 2025/26, finishing as the joint-second top scorer in the league with 10 goals.

Hugo Broos, the outgoing Bafana Bafana head coach and one of Mofokeng's biggest advocates, backed the move as a positive step for his player's development, while making clear he expected the winger to keep working hard and would remain available for support and advice as the transition to European football unfolds.

Filling the Gap at Mayfair

Mofokeng's exit leaves a significant creative void for Orlando Pirates to fill ahead of the 2026/27 season. The club has already moved to strengthen other areas of the squad through a wide-ranging recruitment drive, but replacing the output of a player who contributed 29 goals and 31 assists across two seasons — while also carrying much of the creative burden in the treble-winning campaign — will not be straightforward. Head coach Abdeslam Ouaddou is expected to rely on a combination of new arrivals and academy graduates to share that attacking responsibility across the new season.

What's Next

Mofokeng is expected to link up with his new Royale Union Saint-Gilloise teammates immediately as the Belgian side begins its Champions League qualifying campaign. Orlando Pirates, meanwhile, will need to rebuild their attack ahead of the 2026/27 Betway Premiership season and their own continental campaign following a historic treble-winning year.
