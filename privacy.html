Privacy Policy

Effective: June 2026

Matchday Soccer Planet ("we", "us", "our") is an independent South African football website operated from Ga-Rankuwa, Pretoria. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from visitors, how we use it, and the choices available to you. We built this site to be as lightweight and privacy-respecting as possible, and the vast majority of the data described below is stored locally in your own browser rather than on a central server we control.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you interact with the site: your name and email address if you register as a member, any comments or reactions you post on articles or the homepage Fan Zone, and your entries into competitions such as our World Cup Predictor. We do not require registration to read any article on this site, and browsing our content does not require you to submit any personal information at all.

2. How Data Is Stored

Member details, comments, penalty-game scores and leaderboard entries are stored using your browser's local storage rather than in a central database. This means your data lives on your own device and browser profile; clearing your browser data will remove it. If you use multiple devices or browsers, your Matchday Soccer Planet activity will not automatically carry over between them.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We use essential cookies required for the site to function, and, where you have provided consent via our cookie banner, cookies associated with Google AdSense for the display of advertising. Google may use these cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google's own Ads Settings, or by adjusting your browser's cookie preferences.

4. How We Use Your Information

Any information you provide is used solely to operate the features you engage with — displaying your comments and reactions, running our community leaderboard, and processing competition entries. We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights Under POPIA

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. Because most data on this site is stored in your own browser, you can typically exercise these rights yourself by clearing your browser's local storage for this site. For any other requests, or questions about this policy, please contact us at matchdaysoccerplanet@gmail.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the site evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
